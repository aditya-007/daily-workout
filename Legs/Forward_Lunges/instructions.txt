Exercise Name: Forward Lunges
Muscle Group: Quads (Legs)
Equipment: Bodyweight
Difficulty: Novice
Model: Female
Source: https://musclewiki.com/hi-in/exercise/forward-lunges?model=f

Note: MuscleWiki's hi-in (Hindi) locale only translates the site UI text;
the step-by-step exercise instructions are not localized into Hindi at the
source (they load in English even on this Hindi URL). English is provided
below as the best available fallback, as requested.

Instructions:
1
एक पैर आगे बढ़ाएँ।
2
अपने शरीर को नीचे उतारें जब तक पीछे वाला घुटना लगभग जमीन को छू ले।
3
सीधा रहें और सुनिश्चित करें कि सामने वाला घुटना सामने वाले पैर के ऊपर रहे।
4
सामने वाले पैर से ज़मीन को धकेलें और प्रारंभिक स्थिति में लौटें। पैर बदलें।

1. Step forward with one leg.
2. Lower your body until your rear knee nearly touches the ground.
3. Ensure you remain upright, and your front knee stays above the front foot.
4. Push off the floor with your front foot until you return to the starting position. Switch legs.
