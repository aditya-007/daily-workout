Exercise Name: Side Lunges
Muscle Group: Quads (Legs)
Equipment: Bodyweight
Difficulty: Novice
Model: Female
Source: https://musclewiki.com/hi-in/exercise/side-lunges?model=f

Note: MuscleWiki's hi-in (Hindi) locale only translates the site UI text;
the step-by-step exercise instructions are not localized into Hindi at the
source (they load in English even on this Hindi URL). English is provided
below as the best available fallback, as requested.

Instructions:
1
धीरे से दाईं ओर एक साइड स्टेप लें।
2
बाएं घुटने को सीधा करें और अपना वजन दाईं तरफ ले जाएँ।
3
घुटना मोड़ें और शरीर को नीचे ले जाएँ।
4
धक्का देकर प्रारंभिक स्थिति में लौटें।

1. Take a slow, lateral step to the right.
2. Extend the left knee, and drive your weight into your right side.
3. Flex your knee and lower your body.
4. Push back to the starting position.
