Exercise Name: Walking Lunge
Muscle Group: Quads (Legs)
Equipment: Bodyweight
Difficulty: Intermediate
Model: Female
Source: https://musclewiki.com/hi-in/exercise/lunge-walking?model=f

Note: MuscleWiki's hi-in (Hindi) locale only translates the site UI text;
the step-by-step exercise instructions are not localized into Hindi at the
source (they load in English even on this Hindi URL). English is provided
below as the best available fallback, as requested.

Instructions:
1
एक कदम आगे बढ़ाएँ और अपना वजन आगे वाली टांग पर डालें।
2
आगे वाले पैर का घुटना मोड़ें ताकि पीछे वाले पैर का घुटना जमीन के करीब आ जाए।
3
खड़े हो जाएँ और दूसरे पैर से लंज में जाएँ।

1. Take a step forward shifting your weight onto the front leg.
2. Bend the knee of the front leg so the knee of the back leg comes close to the ground.
3. Stand up and go into lunging with the opposite leg.
