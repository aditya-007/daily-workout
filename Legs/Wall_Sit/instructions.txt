Exercise Name: Wall Sit
Muscle Group: Quads (Legs)
Equipment: Bodyweight
Difficulty: Novice
Model: Female
Source: https://musclewiki.com/hi-in/exercise/wall-sit?model=f

Note: MuscleWiki's hi-in (Hindi) locale only translates the site UI text;
the step-by-step exercise instructions are not localized into Hindi at the
source (they load in English even on this Hindi URL). English is provided
below as the best available fallback, as requested.

Instructions:
1
अपनी पीठ दीवार के साथ टिका कर रखें, पैरों को कंधे की चौड़ाई पर रखें।
2
स्क्वाट पोज़िशन में नीचे जाएँ, जांघें फर्श के समानांतर हों और घुटने टखनों के ऊपर रहें।
3
स्थिति बनाए रखें, कोर और पैरों में तनाव बनाए रखें।

1. Press your back against a wall, feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Lower into a squat position, thighs parallel to the floor, knees above ankles.
3. Hold the position, maintaining tension in your core and legs.
