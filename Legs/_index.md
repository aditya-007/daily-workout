Source category: https://musclewiki.com/hi-in/exercises/quads/bodyweight?model=f (pages 1-5, 20 exercises)

**Important note on language:** The `hi-in` locale on MuscleWiki only translates
the surrounding site UI (menus, pagination, footer). The exercise step-by-step
instructions themselves are served in English even on the Hindi URLs — MuscleWiki
has not localized that content yet. All instructions below/in each folder are
therefore in English (the best available data), as agreed.

Each exercise has its own folder under `Lower body/` containing `instructions.txt`.
Add your own `animation.mp4` / `animation.gif` into the matching folder.
